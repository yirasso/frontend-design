Seven books. Thirty-one days. Then we empty the shelf.

A bookshop with one shelf on it. We stock seven titles, we can defend all seven, and on the 7th of each month they go back in the boxes whether you got to them or not.

03 — Why seven

A shop with twelve thousand books is a warehouse holding opinions it will not say out loud. We keep seven, we have read all seven, and if you ask why the seventh is there we will tell you at length.

The house rules, all of them
  1. Sit as long as you like. Nobody will come over.
  2. No laptops after five. Notebooks always.
  3. The coffee is free after six and it is not very good.
  4. You may read a book you have not bought. That is the entire point.
  5. Talk quietly, or take it to the courtyard, which is nicer anyway.
