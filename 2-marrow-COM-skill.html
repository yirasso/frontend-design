01 Why three

A roastery with forty coffees is a warehouse.

We buy two or three bags at a time, from importers who will tell us what the producer was actually paid. Three lots fit on the shelf and in our heads. When one runs out we don’t reorder it — the next harvest isn’t the same coffee, and pretending otherwise is how everything on a shelf ends up tasting like chocolate and nuts.

So the list changes because the year changed. Sometimes that means the coffee you liked in March is not coming back. We think that is the interesting part.

Around eight minutes the bean splits — loud, like rain on a tin roof. Almost everything that makes a coffee taste like that coffee happens in the ninety seconds after. We drop between 55 and 80 seconds past the crack. Nothing here goes dark; if you want burnt there are four places on this street.
