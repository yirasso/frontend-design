(01) The idea Est. MMXIX

Most roasters give you forty coffees and no opinion. We give you three, and we will happily argue about them.

Marrow started in a garage off Rua da Boavista with one drum roaster, a borrowed grinder and a stubborn belief that a bean has one best version of itself — and that the roaster’s only job is to find it and get out of the way.

We buy small. A lot is thirty to sixty bags, bought from producers we can name, at prices we publish. When it’s gone, it’s gone, and something else takes its place on the bench. Three at a time. No more, because three is what we can taste properly every single week.

(04) Method Bean to doorstep

Four things, done slowly.

Sourced

We buy from eleven producers we’ve met. Contracts are annual, prices published on every bag.

Roasted

Tuesdays, on a restored 1957 Probat. Twelve-kilo batches, profiled to the lot, never to a schedule.

Rested

Eight days in a sealed bin. Degassing is not a delay — it’s the difference between sharp and sweet.

Posted

Bagged with a one-way valve and in the post the same morning. Most of Portugal has it next day.
