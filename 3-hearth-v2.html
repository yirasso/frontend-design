What it is

Not a library of tracks. A thing that plays.

Every other option ships you finished audio and asks you to fade between clips. Hearth ships the composer instead: a 2.4 MB engine that synthesises each note the moment before you hear it.

01

Synthesis, not playback

No stems, no crossfades, no seams. Voices are generated per-sample against a harmonic model, so a session can run for nine hours without a repeat.

02

Reads the room

Feed it anything — scroll depth, occupancy, time of day, a heart rate. Hearth maps signals to density, register and decay rather than to track selection.

03

Yours outright

Output is generated on your device, so there is nothing to clear and no one to pay per play. Export any passage to stems if you need it fixed in place.

Method

Three moving parts.

  1. A harmonic field

    You choose a tonal centre and a temperament. Hearth holds a slow-moving field of consonance around it and never leaves the space you defined.

  2. A conductor

    A small model decides what should enter, thin out or fall silent, on a horizon of about forty seconds. It is deterministic: the same seed and the same inputs give you the same performance, every time.

  3. A voice bank

    Fourteen synthesised instruments, each with a physical model rather than a sample set, so pitch and dynamics stay honest at the extremes.
