03 — What it records

Ice is not a metaphor for memory. It is a physical recording medium, and it holds four channels at once.

  1. 01

    Trapped air

    Bubbles sealed when the snow closed over. Not a proxy, not a model — the actual atmosphere of that year, measurable by the same instruments we point at today's.

    ppm CO₂ / CH₄
  2. 02

    Oxygen isotopes

    The ratio of ¹⁸O to ¹⁶O in the water itself falls with the temperature of the cloud it fell from. Winter and summer alternate visibly, which is what makes counting years possible at all.

    δ¹⁸O ‰
  3. 03

    Ash and dust

    Every large eruption leaves a dark band with a chemical fingerprint. These are the fixed points — a layer matched to Laki dates everything above and below it.

    particles / mL
  4. 04

    What we put there

    Lead from Roman smelting. Fallout from atmospheric weapons testing, sharpest in 1963. Sulphates that climb from 1850 and fall again after 1990. The record does not editorialise.

    ng / kg
04 — The vault

A freezer is a library with one very strict rule.

Sections are stacked in aluminium trays, by depth, in the order they came out of the ground. There is no reshelving and no reordering — the sequence is the data. Lose it and the ice becomes ordinary frozen water.

Two staff are inside at any time, in twenty-minute shifts. The building has never lost power for longer than nine minutes.

05 — Access

We will cut you a piece, and then it is gone forever.

Every analysis destroys the sample. That is not a caveat, it is the whole difficulty: the archive is finite and shrinks each time it is useful. Requests are read four times a year.

  1. 01

    Institutional affiliation, or a named collaborator who has one.

  2. 02

    A method that cannot be run on ice we have already melted and archived as water.

  3. 03

    Depths, in metres, with a tolerance. "Around the Younger Dryas" is not a depth.

  4. 04

    Agreement to publish the null result if that is what you get.
